# Privacy Policy — Badge Studio

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Badge Studio stores the merchant's shop domain, Shopify authentication/session information, and badge configuration, including selected Shopify product IDs and titles. It does not intentionally collect or store customer personal data.

Shopify provides data needed to install, authenticate, and operate the app. Badge configuration is stored only to provide the service. Authentication records and badge configuration are deleted when Shopify sends an app-uninstalled or shop-redaction request. Customer data access and redaction requests are acknowledged; the app has no customer records to return or erase.

Service providers used for production hosting and database storage may process this limited merchant data on our behalf. Data is retained while the app is installed and only as needed for security, legal, and operational obligations afterward.
